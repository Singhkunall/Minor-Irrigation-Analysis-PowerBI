Project Report & Documentation

Download Full PDF Report

This Minor Irrigation Analysis project provides a comprehensive data-driven study of irrigation infrastructure, crop season dependencies, and groundwater sustainability across Uttar Pradesh villages. The primary objective is to transform raw survey data into actionable insights for agricultural planners and policymakers using Microsoft Power BI.

  • Evaluate regional disparities in net sown vs irrigated areas.
  • Analyze seasonal crop trends across Kharif, Rabi, and Perennial periods.
  • Monitor pre-monsoon and post-monsoon groundwater level fluctuations.
  • Assess the distribution and impact of Water User Associations (WUAs).

The core dataset consists of 106,402 village records (`UPVillageSchedule.csv`) containing 19 distinct attribute columns covering geography, crop areas, irrigation modes, groundwater depths, and scheme availability.

Power Query Cleaning Steps

  • Removed redundant header rows and null records.
  • Handled missing values (`NA` replaced with `0` for numeric measures).
  • Standardized district and block spelling conventions.
  • Converted text-formatted numbers into Decimal/Integer types.

Calculated DAX Measures

  • Total Irrigated Area = SUM(Gross_Irrigated_Total)
  • Irrigation Efficiency % = DIVIDE(Net_Irrigated, Cultivable_Area)
  • Groundwater Level Change = Avg(Post_Monsoon) - Avg(Pre_Monsoon)
High Seasonal Dependency: Rabi season irrigation accounts for the highest water consumption (41.7%), heavily relying on tube-wells and groundwater, whereas Kharif season benefits partially from monsoon rains.
Regional Disparities: Districts like Kheri, Aligarh, and Bareilly lead with over 500,000 hectares of irrigated coverage, while southern districts show lower net irrigation coverage relative to cultivable land.
Groundwater Table Recharge: Post-monsoon groundwater levels demonstrate an average recovery of ~0.12 meters state-wide, but deep water tables (>15m) in districts like Prayagraj and Firozabad demand artificial recharge structures.
